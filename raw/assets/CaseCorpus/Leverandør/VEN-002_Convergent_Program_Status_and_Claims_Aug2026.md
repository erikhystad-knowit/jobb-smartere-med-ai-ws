# Convergent Program Status and Claims – August 2026

## Executive position
The migration tool was delivered five weeks late, three critical integrations required supplier rework, and two solution architect transitions reduced continuity. Convergent accepts that these items affected the plan.

## Customer dependencies
Nordflow process decisions arrived four to nine weeks after requested dates. Requirements changed after configuration, and representative billing test data was unavailable for several cycles. Of the 18 integrations not production-ready, Convergent classifies five as supplier defects, ten as customer decision/data dependencies and three as shared rework.

## Commercial proposal
Convergent is prepared to discuss credits in the range NOK 24–34 million if scope is frozen and a jointly governed plan is agreed. This paper is a supplier position and does not constitute an agreed root-cause allocation.
